Outbreak Response

A cooperative pathogen-fighting game for 2 to 6 players.
Work together. Treat, prevent, and cure disease before the world falls.

or
How to play (click to expand)

Goal

Work together as a health response team to cure all four kinds of pathogen before 3 regions fall.

Pathogen categories

  • Virus — treated with antivirals and rest; prevented with vaccines and masks.
  • Bacteria — treated with antibiotics; prevented with hygiene and safe food.
  • Fungus — treated with antifungals; prevented by keeping skin dry and clean.
  • Parasite — treated with antiparasitics; prevented with clean water and insect control.

On your turn

  1. Move to an adjacent region (costs 1 AP).
  2. Treat an infection by correctly answering a pathogen quiz (2 AP, 1 AP for Doctor).
  3. Prevent by running a public-health campaign (2 AP, 1 AP for Nurse). Creates a shield.
  4. Share Intel with a teammate in the same region to give them bonus AP next turn.
  5. End your turn. The team earns research only by answering correctly.

When all players have played, new infections appear. A category is cured at 4 research tokens.

Win / lose

Win: cure all 4 pathogen categories. Lose: 3 regions fall, or 12 rounds pass.
